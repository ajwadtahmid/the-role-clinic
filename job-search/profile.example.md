# Candidate profile (template)

Copy this file to `profile.md` and fill it in. `profile.md` is not committed. The job search agent
reads it before every run, together with `resume.*`. Delete lines that do not apply.

## Target roles
- Level: (for example entry-level, 0-2 years; up to about 3 only if projects or related experience count)
- Employment: full-time only (exclude internships, co-ops, contract, temporary and part-time)
- Interests: (list role families, most wanted first)

## Facts not on the résumé
- Work authorization: (citizenship, visa status, and whether roles needing clearance or citizenship are allowed)
- Location: (home area, whether relocation, remote, hybrid and on-site are acceptable)
- Salary: (report only, or filter below a number)
- Certifications held: (and how to treat a missing required or preferred one)
- Graduation date: (shared or not; how to treat roles gated by a graduation window)

## Flags to record in "Missing Skills / Flags"
Citizenship or clearance requirements, graduation-window limits, overnight or rotating shifts, heavy
travel, on-site requirements, state-residency limits, no-sponsorship language, and any requirement the
résumé does not meet.

## Priority notes
- What does not lower priority (for example clearance or on-site location)?
- Which limits force Low priority (for example a graduation window)?

## Preferences learned over time
- (for example "avoid on-site", "too senior at Company X")
